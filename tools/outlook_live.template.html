Outlook (live, experimental): where hurricane-force and storm-force lows may occur

Experimental. Not a warning and not an OPC product. Rebuilt once a day from the newest 00 UTC GFS run. GFS gust is a model forecast and a proxy for the sustained wind OPC warns on, and OPC’s hurricane-force call is an analyst’s judgement, so “OPC agreed” is not the same as “the wind will be there”. How to read it

Each shaded area is the chance that OPC had a hurricane-force (or storm-force) low within 324 nautical miles of a point on that forecast day, given the strongest forecast GFS gust near the point. “Near” is a set distance that grows with lead: 60 nautical miles on Day 1, 120 on Day 2, 270 on Days 3 and 4 (the smallest that caught at least 80% of hurricane-force gust area when the model low was out of place, ERA5 proxy, position error only). Areas are drawn from the model’s own wind field, so a storm the tracker misses still shows.

No shading is not “safe”: it means GFS gust stayed in a band where OPC agreed under 10% of the time, a lower chance, not a guarantee.

What is real, what is not

Wind measure. OPC warns on sustained wind. Our line is a GFS gust, a proxy, and OPC’s hurricane-force call is an analyst’s judgement, so “OPC agreed” is not “the wind will be there”.

Not on this page. No storm centres or pressures, because the shading is tracker-free. No 500 hPa trough, 250 hPa jet or coupled hatch, because those need extra GFS fields this page does not pull. No gale shading, because there is no OPC truth for gale. Nothing past Day 4, because the rates stop at 96 h.

Limits

The percentages come from five past seasons (October–April 2021–22 to 2025–26) of 00 UTC runs of one model that changed version inside the window, and hold only for that sample, so read them as rates, not precise odds. Gust is sampled every 6 hours, so a short peak can be missed. Points are 1 degree apart and in the same run they are not independent: the real sample is runs and seasons. The tables here use all five seasons, so unlike the replay page they were not built without the run being shaded. The shading radii come from a position-error test on the ERA5 proxy. Land and coasts are not scored. Nothing beyond day 4, nothing from May to September, and the map is rebuilt once a day, so it can be up to a day behind the latest GFS run.
