GFS gust rule

When the GFS forecast gust reaches about 72 kt, how often is OPC calling hurricane force?

This is the raw GFS forecast gust, with no model on top: for each forecast low, the highest ocean gust within 800 km, set against whether OPC’s archive had a hurricane-force fix near the same place and time. Five seasons (2021-22 to 2025-26), October to April, forecasts from 00 and 12 UTC, 0 to 48 hours ahead; 90,131 forecast fixes. The cut-off that matches OPC’s hurricane-force counts is about 72 kt (71.5 to 72.5 kt depending on the season held out).

Read it as odds, not a switch. A bigger gust does not make OPC’s call certain: the share peaks near two in three and does not reach 100%. No gust band is a sure thing.

“Tropical-linked removed” drops forecast fixes within 400 km of a hurricane or tropical-storm track point (IBTrACS) at the same time. That cut was added after seeing the first table, so treat it as a post hoc check; it changes little. The matching is a first look, not a tightened tropical definition. The two highest bands hold few lows, so their shares are rough.

OPC is not a measurement. Its call is an analyst’s judgement of all the data available, and can lean towards caution for customers, so it can sit above or below the wind the model shows. A GFS gust with no OPC call is not necessarily a GFS error, and a missed OPC call may be a GFS under-forecast or an OPC over-call. This table cannot tell which: that needs observed winds (scatterometer, ships, buoys). “OPC agreed” means OPC also called it, not that the forecast was correct.

At about 72 kt the forecast catches roughly half of OPC’s hurricane-force lows and OPC agrees with about half of its calls. Lowering the cut to 64 kt catches about nine in ten but OPC agrees with only about three in ten of the calls. ERA5 proxy lows are not used here: these are GFS forecast fixes found by the same tracker, scored against OPC’s archive. Five seasons; fixes within one storm are not independent, so no intervals are shown. The “72 kt” cut-off was matched to OPC counts on these same seasons, one season held out at a time. Why the share stops rising above about 85 kt is not known.
