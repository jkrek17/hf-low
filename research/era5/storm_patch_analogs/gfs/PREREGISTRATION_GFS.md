# GFS test of the storm-centred pattern terms: pre-registration

Written 2026-10-14, **before any GFS field was read**. The frozen ERA5 PCA and models were fitted first (`fit_frozen.py`, hashes below, committed with this file). ERA5 pipeline A is a **proxy**: it supplies the training record and the truth. Nothing here is validated against observations. Question from Jason (Q&A thread): does the +0.024 outlook skill gain of the 500 nm pattern (research PR 199, audit PR 201; ERA5 patches in, ERA5 outcomes out) survive when the patch comes from a GFS forecast?

## Design

- **Sample:** the matched lead 24 h GFS fixes of PR 185 (`../../gfs_outlook`, `score_outlook_full.py`): GFS tracker fixes (`gfs_test/results/gfs_tracks_2021..2025.csv.gz`), cycles 00/12 UTC 1 Oct to 30 Apr, seasons 2021-22 to 2025-26, valid 00/12 UTC, atl/pac, each matched to the nearest pipeline A fix at the same valid time within 500 km (truth = its four-class next-24 h gust class, `bomb_env/score.load`); the scored sample is the 18,167 fixes of PR 185 that have every GFS and ERA5 environment term (and, new here, a finite patch). Scored on the same fixes: counts will be printed before scoring.
- **Patch:** centred on the GFS tracker fix at lead 24 (valid time = cycle + 24 h); 13 x 13 points, 154.33 km spacing, north-up; channels 500 hPa height anomaly (m), sea-level pressure anomaly (PRMSL, hPa), 250 hPa u and v anomaly (m/s) from the GFS 0.25 degree forecast file f024 (`noaa-gfs-bdp-pds`), coarsened to 1.5 degrees with `intensity/env.py` `coarsen` (the ERA5 pipeline's own regrid rule) and sampled with the same bilinear code as the ERA5 patches. Anomalies are relative to the **same WeatherBench2 1990-2019 ERA5 climatology** as the training patches (`/mnt/project-files/storm-patches/clim_patch_0012.npz`). A GFS-vs-ERA5 climate or bias difference therefore enters as an anomaly offset; it is reported (below) but not corrected in the primary test.
- **Frozen artefacts** (fitted before any GFS read; `results/frozen_*`): scaler, patch mean and 15-component PCA on ERA5 pipeline A fixes of seasons 2014-15 to 2020-21 (28,293 fixes; 2021-22 left out because it is also a GFS season), and the models FULL7 (the PR 185 FULL predictors: 8 state, 3 Hart, 10 environment; dp12 model with a no-dp12 fallback, as PR 185), FULLPC7 (FULL7 + 15 components) and FULLPC7_OUT (FULL7 + 15 components of the patch with the inner 300 km removed), all `fit_models.build`, C = 1. sha256 (first 16 hex): frozen_pca_full.npz d1215a13a285c12c, frozen_pca_out.npz 19c35ee19947363f, frozen_models.json 1905cfc31eebc2ef. No refit, recalibration or bias correction on GFS data in the registered tests.
- **GFS-fed inputs** of FULL7 are the PR 185 tables (`gfs_outlook_env.csv.gz`, `gfs_outlook_env_full.csv.gz`) and GFS tracker fields; nothing new is pulled for them. Fixes without the full GFS terms are dropped exactly as in PR 185.
- **Scores:** RPSS against the basin-month climatology of `outlook_models_full.json` (2004-2020, as PR 185); season-block bootstrap over the 5 GFS seasons (`score_outlook.draws`, 2,000 draws, seed 20261013), 90 % intervals. With 5 seasons there are at most 126 distinct resamples; the interval describes season-to-season variation in this set, not a future season, and p values floor near 1/2001.

## Tests

- **T1 (primary):** RPSS(FULLPC7 fed GFS) minus RPSS(FULL7 fed GFS), lead 24, pooled basins, same fixes. Rule, written now: **"survives"** if the gain is >= +0.010 and the 90 % lower bound > 0; **"no useful gain"** if the upper bound < +0.005; otherwise **"cannot tell"**. Expectation: positive but smaller than the ERA5 gain (+0.024 LOSO, PR 199); I expect +0.005 to +0.020, because GFS fields at 24 h carry forecast error and the climatology offset.
- **S1 (outer pattern):** the same with FULLPC7_OUT (inner 300 km removed). Registered family: T1 and S1 only (BH over 2; T1 is the headline).
- **Descriptive, not tested:** (a) placebo: GFS patches permuted among fixes of the same basin, calendar month and valid hour (seed 20261014), frozen model unchanged; (b) gain by season, basin and class threshold; (c) the existing FULL model (2004-2020 fit) GFS-fed RPSS on the same fixes for reference (PR 185 +0.3509); (d) fixes with no other GFS track within 800 km at the valid time; (e) **ERA5-fed comparison on the one overlap season:** for GFS lead 24 fixes of 2021-22 whose matched ERA5 fix has an ERA5 patch (PR 199 product), the gain fed the ERA5 patch (frozen models; 2021-22 is outside their training) versus fed the GFS patch on the same fixes. One season, so a retention ratio from it is an illustration, not a result; (f) GFS minus ERA5 mean and SD of each patch channel on those same fixes (bias diagnostic).
- **Post hoc if wanted, labelled:** per-channel GFS mean-bias removal before projection.

## Size and gates

Four records per cycle (HGT 500 mb, PRMSL, UGRD and VGRD 250 mb) at f024 of 2,122 cycles, byte ranges by `.idx` (method of `gfs_test/a_fetch.py`). Probed from the `.idx` files and HEAD of 30 random cycles (no field read): 2.84 to 3.06 MB per cycle (HGT 0.81, PRMSL 0.97, UGRD 0.59, VGRD 0.60 MB), **6.3 GB for all 2,122 cycles**, under the 50 GB gate; the extractor stops at 9 GB. Nothing in the existing GFS tables covers it (earlier pulls kept per-fix environment tables, not grids). Output: one compact file of float16 patches (about 20 MB) in `/mnt/project-files/storm-patches/gfs/`, not in git.

## Limits stated now

ERA5 proxy truth; five GFS seasons all inside the block that calibrated the 71.7 kt gust cut; operational GFS changed versions across 2021-26; the GFS anomaly uses an ERA5 climatology; the GFS tracker position is the patch centre (it can be a forecast position error away from the true low); the GFS-fed matched sample excludes storms the proxy did not track; GFS `PRMSL` is not the same reduction as ERA5 `msl`. Read as "does the frozen pattern term still help when fed forecast fields", not as a validated product.
