== E. Per-season gains and interval methods
  per-season gain: 2014-15 +0.0196, 2015-16 +0.0268, 2016-17 +0.0245, 2017-18 +0.0281, 2018-19 +0.0191, 2019-20 +0.0269, 2020-21 +0.0250, 2021-22 +0.0212

  leave-one-season-out pooled gain: +0.0233 .. +0.0246
  90% interval, method 1 (as reported): resample 8 seasons with replacement 2000 times, pool the per-season loss sums, 5-95 percentile: +0.0219 .. +0.0258
  90% interval, method 2: t interval on the 8 per-season gains (t7 = 1.89): +0.0216 .. +0.0262
  Both treat seasons as independent and fixed the model (15 PCs, k = 30, C = 1) before the data were read; neither includes uncertainty from the ERA5 proxy or from choices not tried. Within a season the 4,000 fixes are strongly autocorrelated, which is why seasons, not fixes, are resampled.
