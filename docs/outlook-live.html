Experimental. Not a warning and not an OPC product. This map is rebuilt once a day from the newest 00 UTC GFS run. GFS gust is a model forecast and a proxy for the sustained wind OPC warns on, and OPC’s hurricane-force call is an analyst’s judgement, so “OPC agreed” is not the same as “the wind will be there”. For warnings use OPC’s own products.

In plain words
  • What the colours mean. Pick a point. Look at the strongest forecast GFS gust within a set distance of it during that forecast day (60 nautical miles on day 1, 120 on day 2, 270 on days 3 and 4). In five past seasons (October–April, 2021–22 to 2025–26), when the gust was in the same band, OPC had a hurricane-force low within 324 nautical miles of the point on the percentage the colour shows.
  • It is a neighbourhood, not one storm. The colours come straight from the GFS wind field, not from tracking a low, so a storm the tracker would have lost still shows.
  • No colour is not “safe”. Uncoloured ocean means gust stayed in a band where OPC agreed under 1% of the time. It is a lower chance, not a guarantee, and the model can miss a storm.
  • Even the strongest gusts are not a sure thing. Where the GFS gust was 80 kt or more, OPC had a hurricane-force low nearby on about 58% of past point-days on day 1 and about 39% by day 4. Chances fall with lead time.

Limits. The percentages come from five past seasons (October–April 2021–22 to 2025–26) of 00 UTC runs of one model that changed version inside the window, and hold only for that sample, so read them as rates, not precise odds. Gust is sampled every 6 hours, so a short peak can be missed. Points are 1 degree apart and in the same run they are not independent: the real sample is runs and seasons. The tables here use all five seasons, so unlike the replay page they were not built without the run being shaded. The shading radii come from a position-error test on the ERA5 proxy. Land and coasts are not scored. Nothing beyond day 4, nothing from May to September, and the map is rebuilt once a day, so it can be up to a day behind the latest GFS run.

Method, calibration and how past runs lined up with OPC: Outlook replay. Code: tools/outlook_live.py (daily job) and research/era5/outlook_replay/ in the repository.
